Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QLabX handles information in connection with CAPY: Guardian of the Springs (“CAPY”, “the Game”, or “the App”).

Short version: CAPY is designed to be playable without creating an account. QLabX does not intentionally collect, sell, or share players’ personal information through the Game. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player’s device unless a platform feature explicitly provides its own cloud or account service.

1. Information We Collect

QLabX does not intentionally collect personal information directly through CAPY. The Game does not require your name, email address, phone number, home address, precise location, contacts, or government identifiers.

The Game does not require a QLabX account in order to play.

2. Local Game Data

CAPY may store data locally on your device to provide normal game functionality, including:

  • save files and game progress;
  • settings, language, audio levels, and control preferences;
  • unlocked levels, worlds, items, abilities, achievements, or similar gameplay state;
  • temporary cache files needed for performance.

This data is not transmitted to QLabX unless a future version clearly introduces an online feature that requires such transmission and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly.

3. Device Permissions

CAPY only uses device permissions when required for a gameplay or technical feature. Depending on the version and platform, this may include basic functionality such as vibration or network access if an online platform feature is used.

CAPY does not require access to contacts, SMS messages, call history, precise location, microphone, or camera for its core gameplay. If a future feature requires such access, the operating system will request the relevant permission and this policy will be updated.

4. Internet and Network Access

The core game experience is intended to work without requiring a player account. If the Game accesses the internet, it may do so for platform-related functions such as store delivery, update checks, cloud functionality, achievements, or other services provided by the distribution platform.

QLabX does not use network access to secretly collect personal information.

5. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

QLabX does not use CAPY to sell personal data or build advertising profiles about players. The privacy approach of the Game is to avoid unnecessary tracking.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar third-party technology is added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the applicable store data-safety disclosures will be updated.

6. Google Play and Other Platform Providers

When you download or use CAPY through Google Play or another distribution platform, that platform may independently process information such as account information, device information, purchase history, diagnostics, security signals, or download statistics.

Such processing is controlled by the relevant platform provider and governed by its own privacy policy and terms. QLabX does not control data processed independently by Google Play, Android, device manufacturers, or other platform services.

7. Children’s Privacy

CAPY does not intentionally request personal information from children. Because QLabX does not operate user accounts or intentionally collect personal information through the Game, we do not knowingly create personal profiles of children.

Parents or guardians who believe personal information has somehow been provided to QLabX in connection with the Game may contact us using the developer contact information shown on the official store listing.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since gameplay data is primarily stored locally, you can usually remove it by using the Game’s reset/delete options where available, clearing the App’s storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the Game.

QLabX cannot delete information held independently by Google, an operating-system provider, a device manufacturer, or another third-party platform.

9. Security

We aim to minimize the amount of data handled by the Game and use reasonable technical measures to protect the integrity of the App. No software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but collecting less personal information reduces unnecessary privacy risk.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or complaint to a competent data-protection authority.

Because CAPY is designed not to maintain player accounts or intentionally collect personal information, QLabX may have no personal data linked to you to provide, correct, or delete.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CAPY changes, when new platform features are introduced, or when legal requirements change. The updated version will show a revised effective date.
